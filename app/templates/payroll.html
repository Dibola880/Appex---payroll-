Payroll Processing

Appex Payroll uses the employee's monthly salary as the basic salary and allows additional earnings and deductions to be entered for each payroll run.

After processing, the system creates a payroll run and individual payslips for each active employee.

Employees can then access their payslips from their employee portal.
