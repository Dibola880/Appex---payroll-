How accounting periods are created

  • The first and last periods may be partial months when your financial year starts or ends mid-month.
  • Your company's standard chart of accounts will be reconciled without intentionally replacing existing account customisations.
  • Existing financial-year configurations are checked to prevent duplicate setup.
