How Loan Repayment Works

1. Application

Submit your loan application through Appex Payroll.

2. Employer Review

Your employer reviews the application and determines whether it will be approved.

3. Disbursement

Once approved and marked as disbursed, the loan becomes active.

4. Payroll Deduction

The scheduled repayment can be deducted automatically from payroll when the payroll run is completed.

5. Loan Repaid

Once the outstanding balance reaches zero, the loan status changes to Repaid.
